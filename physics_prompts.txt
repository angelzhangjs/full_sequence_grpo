# Physics-Based Text-to-Video Prompts
# Demonstrating Dynamic Motions and Physical Properties

# ============================================================================
# GRAVITY & FREE FALL
# ============================================================================
A red apple falls from a tree branch, accelerating downward and landing on grass below.
A feather and a bowling ball drop simultaneously in a vacuum chamber, falling at the same rate.
A water droplet falls from a faucet, stretching into a teardrop shape before splashing on the surface.
A basketball drops from shoulder height, bounces several times with decreasing height, and rolls to a stop.

# ============================================================================
# MOMENTUM & COLLISIONS
# ============================================================================
Two billiard balls collide head-on, the moving ball stops completely while the stationary ball rolls forward.
A Newton's cradle with five silver balls, the end ball swings and transfers momentum through the chain.
A bowling ball strikes ten pins, scattering them outward in different directions with varying speeds.
Two identical toy cars collide head-on at equal speeds and bounce backward symmetrically.
A large steel ball and a small marble collide, the marble bounces away at high speed while the ball barely moves.

# ============================================================================
# PROJECTILE MOTION
# ============================================================================
A soccer ball is kicked at 45 degrees, following a parabolic arc through the air before landing.
A basketball arcs through the air in a perfect parabola, swishing through the hoop.
Water streams from a garden hose in a parabolic trajectory, landing in a flower bed several feet away.
A tennis ball is hit with topspin, curving downward in flight and bouncing forward sharply.

# ============================================================================
# CIRCULAR & ROTATIONAL MOTION
# ============================================================================
A yo-yo spins at the end of its string, then climbs back up as the string winds.
A figure skater spins with arms extended, then pulls arms in and spins much faster.
A spinning top wobbles in precession while rotating rapidly on its tip.
A bicycle wheel spins freely, gradually slowing down due to air resistance.
A Ferris wheel rotates smoothly at constant speed, carrying passengers in circular motion.

# ============================================================================
# PENDULUM & OSCILLATION
# ============================================================================
A pendulum swings back and forth with decreasing amplitude, gradually coming to rest.
A child on a swing moves through large arcs, reaching maximum height at the endpoints.
A spring with a mass attached oscillates up and down in simple harmonic motion.
A grandfather clock pendulum swings with perfect rhythm, ticking each second.

# ============================================================================
# FRICTION & ROLLING
# ============================================================================
A hockey puck glides across smooth ice with minimal friction, traveling in a straight line.
A wooden block slides down a rough inclined plane, slowing due to friction.
A rolling marble gradually slows on a horizontal surface due to rolling resistance.
A car skids on wet pavement with locked wheels, leaving dark tire marks.

# ============================================================================
# FLUID DYNAMICS
# ============================================================================
Smoke rises from incense in smooth laminar flow, then transitions to turbulent swirls.
A drop of food coloring spreads through clear water in beautiful diffusion patterns.
Honey pours from a jar in a thick, viscous stream, coiling on the plate below.
A leaf floats downstream in a river, spinning in eddies and flowing around rocks.
Bubbles rise through a glass of carbonated soda, accelerating as they ascend.

# ============================================================================
# ELASTICITY & DEFORMATION
# ============================================================================
A rubber band stretches when pulled, then snaps back to original shape when released.
A water balloon deforms on impact with the ground, then bursts in slow motion.
A diving board flexes downward under a diver's weight, then springs them upward.
A trampoline mat deforms deeply when a person lands, then rebounds them into the air.

# ============================================================================
# ENERGY TRANSFER & CONSERVATION
# ============================================================================
A marble rolls down a curved track, speeding up as it descends and slowing on the upslope.
A pole vaulter converts running speed into height, arcing over a high bar.
A wrecking ball swings toward a brick wall, transferring momentum on impact.
A roller coaster car accelerates down a steep drop, converting potential to kinetic energy.

# ============================================================================
# ROTATION & ANGULAR MOMENTUM
# ============================================================================
A coin spins on a table, wobbling faster as it loses energy before falling flat.
A frisbee flies through the air with stable rotation, gliding on aerodynamic lift.
A boomerang spins rapidly while following a curved return path.
A basketball spins backward in mid-air, creating backspin that affects the bounce.

# ============================================================================
# MAGNETISM & FORCES
# ============================================================================
Iron filings arrange themselves along invisible magnetic field lines around a bar magnet.
Two magnets snap together with opposite poles attracting across a gap.
A magnetic levitation toy hovers motionless above a magnetic base.
A compass needle oscillates before settling to point north.

# ============================================================================
# WAVES & VIBRATIONS
# ============================================================================
A rope wave travels from end to end when one end is flicked sharply.
Ripples spread in concentric circles from where a pebble drops into still water.
A guitar string vibrates rapidly after being plucked, gradually damping to stillness.
A tuning fork vibrates at high frequency, creating sound waves in the air.

# ============================================================================
# THERMAL & PHASE TRANSITIONS
# ============================================================================
Ice cubes slowly melt in a glass of water, sinking as they shrink.
Steam rises from a hot cup of coffee in gentle, swirling currents.
A candle flame flickers and dances, reacting to subtle air currents.
Frost crystals form and spread across a cold window pane in branching patterns.

# ============================================================================
# COMPLEX INTERACTIONS
# ============================================================================
A double pendulum swings chaotically, with unpredictable but smooth motion.
Dominos fall in sequence, each transferring momentum to the next in a chain reaction.
A Slinky walks down stairs, one coil at a time in a mesmerizing cascade.
A gyroscope resists tilting while spinning rapidly, maintaining its orientation.
